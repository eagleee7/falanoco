The Manifesto

Not made
to match.
Found to move.

Four rules we hold to on every rack — the same four, stitched onto the tag inside each piece.

+
01 — Source

We hunt it ourselves. No bulk buys, no drop-ships — every piece is pulled by hand from bales, closets, and forgotten racks.

+
02 — Sort

Nothing hits the floor unchecked. We wash it, mend it, press it — clean and ready before it's ever yours.

+
03 — One of one

No pre-made rack to reorder from. Every drop starts fresh — once a piece is gone, it's actually gone.

+
04 — Ground

One city, one shop, one crew. Morang is home — everything else is just delivery.
